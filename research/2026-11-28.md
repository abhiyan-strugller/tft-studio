## Verified figures and sources
- **Prayer flag colours and elements** (blue = sky/space, white = air/wind, red = fire, green = water, yellow = earth),
  the belief that the wind carries prayers to spread goodwill and compassion, and that prayers become part of the
  universe as the flags fade: Wikipedia "Prayer flag" + G Adventures "5 things you should know about Tibetan prayer flags".
  I did not claim a left-to-right order, because only one source gave it.
- **Patan**: "also known as Lalitpur, the city of artisans"; repoussé and lost-wax craft; "Krishna Temple with its
  21 gilded spires, built in 1637"; Patan Durbar Square is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Source: Nepal Tourism Board, ntb.gov.np/patan.
- **Bhaktapur**: "Bhadgaon, literally the City of Devotees"; Nyatapola "five storied"; "famous for pottery and
  woodcarving". Source: Nepal Tourism Board, ntb.gov.np/bhaktapur. Juju dhau was NOT used because no source was confirmed.
- **Temple etiquette**: shoes off; "leather articles are prohibited inside some temple areas"; walk clockwise around
  temples/stupas; ask permission before photographing; "some Hindu temples do not allow non Hindus to enter". Sources:
  Nepal Tourism Board trade.ntb.gov.np/travel-essentials/cultural-etiquette + Rough Guides Nepal customs & etiquette.
  The Pashupatinath inner temple being for Hindus only comes from content/tours.json (pashupatinath-darshan).
- Char Dham days/stops, Kedarnath 3,584 m, Satvik meals, helicopter options: tours.json. ABC Poon Hill 3,210 m,
  Chhomrong 2,170 m, ABC 4,130 m, inclusions: tours.json. Patan Day 2 / Bhaktapur Day 3: tours.json (kathmandu-heritage-culture).
