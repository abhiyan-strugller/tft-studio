## Verified figures and sources
- **Druk Wangyel Tshechu, Dochula — 13 December 2026.** ABTO "Tentative Festival Dates 2026"
  (abto.org.bt, row 39: "Druk Wangyel Tshechu, Douchula, Thimphu, 13th December") and
  Wikipedia "Dochula Pass" ("held annually on December 13"). ABTO marks it tentative, so the
  caption says "held every 13 December … ask us about planning around it", with no promise that the
  5-day trip lands on that date.
- **Dochula 108 chortens:** tours.json (bhutan-5d itinerary), plus Wikipedia "Dochula Pass".
- **Mansarovar:** name = mānas ("mind") + sarovara ("lake"). Created through Brahma's mind, and the
  Ramayana's "lake of consciousness". Sacred in Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism and Bon. Pilgrims believe
  bathing in it and circling Kailash cleanses sins. Source: Wikipedia "Lake Manasarovar". The elevation is
  4,586 m there and 4,600 m on our website, so per the rule the **website figure (4,600 m)** is used.
  The 88 km circumference was dropped because it had only one source.
- Tiger's Nest ≈3,120 m, Muktinath 3,800 m, Mardi BC 4,500 m, High Camp 3,590 m: tours.json.
